Core ResponseiPrimary analysis: SPL capability, frequency response, excursion, and headroom.

Maximum SPL OutputiThermal limit: Max SPL at full power (Pe).

Excursion limit: Max SPL if cone hits Xmax.

Max SPL: The lower of thermal and excursion limits.

Frequency ResponseiRaw: The system's acoustic response (Qtc determines shape).

With modifiers: In-room response including room gain and DSP.

SensitivityiAbsolute SPL at 1W/1m.

Shows the system's efficiency across frequency. Reference line is the driver's rated sensitivity.

Cone ExcursioniActual cone movement at selected power.

Xmax line shows driver's mechanical limit.

Headroom to TargetiCan you hit your target SPL?

Positive = achievable. Negative = need more power/Xmax/subs.

Scenario / EnvironmentiDSP filters and room gain modeling. Configure in Advanced panel above.

DSP MagnitudeiCombined DSP effect from signal chain filters (HPF, PEQ, etc.).

DSP PhaseiPhase shift introduced by DSP filters. Important for time alignment.

Room GainiEnvironmental boost from room placement (corner, wall, etc.).

Ported BoxiPort air velocity, turbulence analysis, and cone/port output split.

Port VelocityiAir velocity in port. High velocity causes chuffing noise.

Mach NumberiVelocity as fraction of speed of sound. >0.05 = audible compression.

Reynolds NumberiFlow turbulence indicator. >50,000 = turbulent flow = noise.

Output ContributioniHow output is shared between cone and port at each frequency.

Passive RadiatoriPR excursion analysis and driver/PR sizing comparison.

PR ExcursioniPassive radiator cone movement at selected power.

Excursion ComparisoniDriver vs PR excursion with Xmax limits. Shows if PR is properly sized.

Power LimitsiMaximum power before driver or PR hits excursion limit. Shows which is the bottleneck.

Output ContributioniHow output is shared between cone and PR at each frequency.

Electrical / AmplifieriImpedance, phase, current draw, and amplifier loading characteristics.

ImpedanceiLoad the amplifier sees. Peaks at resonance.

Impedance PhaseiPhase angle of impedance. High angles stress amplifiers.

EPDR (Amp Load)iEquivalent Peak Dissipation Resistance. Shows true amp stress.

Current DrawiRMS current at selected power. Check against amp specs.

Amplifier Load (VA)iApparent power the amp must deliver (V × A).

Thermal DissipationiHeat generated in voice coil at selected power.

MechanicaliCone velocity and acceleration at selected power.

Cone VelocityiSpeed of cone movement. Related to output capability.

Cone AccelerationiRate of velocity change. Higher = more force required.

Time DomainiPhase, group delay, step and impulse response.

PhaseiPhase response. Important for multi-sub alignment.

Group DelayiTime delay vs frequency. >1 cycle at Fb is audible.

Step ResponseiSystem response to a step input. Shows transient behavior.

Impulse ResponseiSystem response to an impulse. Basis of all other responses.

Design ToolsiVolume comparisons, alignment analysis, and power/SPL relationships.

Volume ComparisoniCompare response at different box volumes.

Alignment ComparisoniCompare sealed vs ported response shapes.

Power RequirediPower needed to hit target SPL at each frequency.

Maximum PoweriMaximum usable power before hitting Xmax or thermal limits.

SPL vs PoweriHow SPL increases with power at different frequencies.

Nonlinear (Klippel)iBl and Kms nonlinearity effects. Requires Klippel parameters in driver data.

Bl CompressioniForce factor reduction at high excursion. Causes output compression.

THD EstimateiEstimated harmonic distortion from Bl and Kms nonlinearity.

Bl(x) CurveiForce factor vs displacement. Flat = linear motor.

Kms(x) CurveiSuspension stiffness vs displacement. Rising = progressive.
