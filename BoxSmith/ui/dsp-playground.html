Tips
HPF: Subsonic protection. 24 dB/oct at 15-25 Hz is typical for ported boxes.
PEQ: Parametric EQ for room mode correction. Use narrow Q (2-4) for nulls, wide Q (0.5-1) for broad shaping.
LPF: Low-pass crossover to blend with mains. Usually handled by AVR, but useful for planning.
APF: Allpass for phase alignment with mains. 2nd order at ~Fc/2 gives 90° at crossover.
